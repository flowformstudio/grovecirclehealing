Additional Support

01

Bonus Video: Reframing Your Thoughts

$100 value

In this talk, I will discuss the power of reframing our thoughts and how it can positively impact your life. I will also share how our thoughts and beliefs can shape our experiences and emotions, and how you can shift our perspective to see situations in a more positive light. We will also provide practical techniques and exercises to help reframe negative or limiting thoughts into more empowering and constructive ones. By the end of the talk, you will have a better understanding of how to identify and challenge negative thought patterns, and how to reframe your thinking to promote greater resilience, creativity, and happiness.

02

Bonus Video: Free Will Choice

$100 value

In this talk, I will explore the concept of free will and choice, and how it affects our daily lives. I will discuss the psychological perspectives on free will, and examine the role of choice in shaping our identity and sense of agency. I will also explore how our past experiences, beliefs, and values can influence the choices we make, and how we can cultivate greater awareness and intentionality in our decision-making process. By the end of the talk, you will have a deeper understanding of the relationship between free will and choice, and how you can use this knowledge to make more conscious and fulfilling choices in your life.
