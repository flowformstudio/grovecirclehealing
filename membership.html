What is the Grove Circle Membership?

The Grove Circle Membership is an online community led by Chris Young-Ginzburg, offering monthly interactive classes that support personal growth, intuitive development, and energetic well-being. Each class explores a unique theme through practical teachings, psychic and energy-healing exercises, guided meditation, sound healing, and meaningful group discussion. Members also enjoy monthly Prayer Circles and a growing library of resources to deepen their self-awareness, strengthen their intuition, and feel supported on their journey.
